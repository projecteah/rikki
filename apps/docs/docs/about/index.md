# Behind the Scenes

The idea of making a Markdown note-taking app began in early 2022, and by the second half of 2022 I finally had the chance to build a desktop application based on Electron.

I named it Rikki. In the etymology of CJK characters, it carries the meaning of recording rationally. It sounded nice, and I have kept this name to this day.

Later on, I came to realize that reinventing the wheel did not bring much substantial value for me, because even I myself preferred using Typora and the like as my Markdown file editor. Halfway through development, Rikki was not really serving its purpose. Besides, there were already plenty of full-fledged note-taking apps like Notion and Obsidian at the time.

But I also found that capturing fleeting thoughts was equally difficult, and I had already developed a habit of saving ephemeral news stories back then.
